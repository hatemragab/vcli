## Exit gate

Record `implemented_local`, `external_acceptance_pending`, failures and unimplemented variants separately. Local readiness requires all four persistence/security combinations, safe generation and capture lifecycle. Synthetic Firebase principals cannot certify external Google/Apple/phone or native clients. Production verification/reset stays pending until Phase 05 real-mail acceptance. Missing external evidence preserves local progress without creating operational claims. An unresolved correctness race blocks its affected recipe; record the exact limit on the [status board](../STATUS.md) before Phase 04 consumes it.
