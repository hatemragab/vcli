## Exit gates

Require reviewed P08-A01–A09 records for advertised profiles, target-specific runbooks, safe failure paths, and explicit pending rows in [status](../STATUS.md). Record decisions in [DECISIONS.md](../../docs/DECISIONS.md). All three artifact/runbook targets remain required. Authenticated operator completion is tracked independently, Dokploy first; Coolify/CapRover remain pending until implemented and verified. Do not label the full target catalog complete while required deliverables remain pending, or equate file rendering, image compilation, provider reachability, deployment, and restore acceptance.
